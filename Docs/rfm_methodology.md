# RFM Methodology

## Measures

- **Recency:** days since the most recent completed order at the production snapshot.
- **Frequency:** number of orders during the trailing 12 months.
- **Monetary:** net revenue during the trailing 12 months.

## Scoring

Each measure is transformed into ranked scores. Lower recency receives a stronger score; higher frequency and monetary value receive stronger scores.

The combined score is mapped to commercial segments:

- Champions;
- Loyal Customers;
- Potential Loyalists;
- Need Attention;
- Hibernating;
- Promising;
- At Risk.

## Business Use

RFM is used for descriptive strategy and is not a substitute for churn or CLV:

- RFM explains recent behavior.
- Churn predicts future inactivity.
- CLV predicts future economic contribution.
- Campaign logic combines all three with consent and treatment economics.

## Governance

Segment populations, revenue shares, churn risk and value contribution should be monitored monthly. Score boundaries should be reviewed when the customer distribution shifts materially.
