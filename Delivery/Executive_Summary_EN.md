# Executive Summary

NovaRetail’s customer intelligence platform creates a single decision record for 6,000 synthetic customers by combining recent commercial behavior, digital engagement, RFM segment, predicted 12-month value, calibrated 90-day churn risk, SHAP reason codes and campaign economics.

The portfolio generated TRY 55.6M of net revenue and TRY 11.9M of predicted 12-month gross-margin contribution. The model identifies 2,682 high or critical-risk customers associated with TRY 6.0M of recent revenue exposure.

The calibrated Random Forest churn champion achieves 0.867 ROC-AUC, 0.908 PR-AUC and 1.77x lift in the highest-risk decile. The Random Forest CLV champion achieves TRY 1,258 MAE, 0.564 R² and 0.792 Spearman correlation on a time-based holdout.

The decision engine does not treat risk as an automatic reason to spend. It combines customer value, consent, expected response and treatment cost. It prioritizes 3,501 customer actions, allocates TRY 236K and estimates TRY 547K of incremental margin.

The complete solution includes Power BI, Excel, Tableau, SQL, FastAPI, Streamlit, bilingual executive presentations, a vector HD report, automated tests, data-quality gates, drift monitoring, fairness diagnostics and model documentation.
